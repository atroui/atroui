OG images are your ad on every link share. Most are blurry, off-brand, or unreadable at thumbnail size.

## Dimensions

1200×630 is the standard. Safe zone: keep text and logos inside the center 80% — platforms crop edges.

## Typography

Large, high-contrast titles. Two lines max. If it doesn't read at 200px wide, rewrite it.

## Contrast

Test on both light and dark Twitter/X backgrounds. Semi-transparent overlays behind text help.
