Scope creep kills sprint margins and client trust. Our antidote is a ritual, not willpower.

## The "not in v1" list

Every sprint starts with a written list of things we are **not** building. Clients sign off on it.

## Success metrics upfront

"If we ship X, how do we know it worked?" - one metric, measurable in week one.

## Change requests = new scope

Mid-sprint additions get logged. We either swap something out or quote a follow-up - never silently absorb.

## The weekly checkpoint

Even in a 7-day sprint, day 4 is a "are we still on scope?" async check. Catching drift early saves the deadline.
