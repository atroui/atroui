## Keyword picture (honest)

### What peers actually rank for (weak organic vs brand)

Semrush “top organic keywords” for BD locale is noisy (e.g. Magic → “confetti”; Aceternity → unrelated long-tails). **Organic is not how they win.** They win:

1. Brand / Direct (~48%)
2. Social + GitHub stars (Magic ~23K★)
3. YouTube / Twitter demos
4. Growing AI referrals
5. Some Google for brand + “animated components” adjacency
